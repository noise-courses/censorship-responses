# Grading rubric — Weekly reading responses

**1 point per week, graded for completion.** Per the syllabus, reading
responses are graded 0/1: if you wrote a good-faith response to that week's
readings and pushed it on time, you get the point. Quality of prose, length
beyond the minimum, and whether we agree with you are *not* graded.

## What earns the point (all must hold)

| # | Requirement |
|---|---|
| 1 | A file named `week-NN.md` (two-digit week, e.g. `week-03.md`) at the top level of your response repo. |
| 2 | Pushed before **Tuesday 9:00 a.m. Chicago time** of the week the topic is covered, so others can read and the instructor can fold it into class. |
| 3 | Addresses the readings assigned for *that* week on the course site, not a different week's or an unrelated article. |
| 4 | Contains a `**Questions**` heading (copy the template in the README) followed by at least one or two sentences of questions you had after reading. A summary is optional; the template no longer asks for one. |
| 5 | Written in good faith: your own reaction, not a pasted abstract, not a bare one-liner such as "interesting paper." |
| 6 | If you used an AI tool, say so in the response and note briefly how you prompted it. |

## What earns 0

- No file for that week, or an empty file / untouched template.
- Pushed after the deadline once your grace balance is exhausted (see below).
- Off-topic: does not engage the week's assigned readings.
- Copied text (abstract, another student's response, verbatim AI output with no
  acknowledgment).

## Requirements that gate the score

- **One file per week.** Do not append weeks to a single file; the grader looks
  for one file per week.
- **Filename.** `week-NN.md` is the convention. The autograder also accepts
  `week-N.md` (and is case-insensitive), but please use the two-digit form.
- **Markdown, in the repo.** Not a PDF, not a Google Doc link, not an email.
- **Structure check.** The repo's lint workflow fails a push whose `week-NN.md`
  lacks a `**Questions**` heading. A red check is a hint to fix the file
  before the deadline, not a grade.
- **AI use.** Encouraged for research and drafting, with acknowledgment. Not
  acknowledging it does not by itself cost the point, but see the next item.
- **We may test your understanding live.** Per the syllabus, we reserve the
  right to check that you grasped the readings through pop quizzes, oral
  questions in class, or other means. Write the response so that you could
  defend it.

## Late policy

Due Tuesday 9:00 a.m. Chicago time. Each hour (or fraction) late draws down
the term's 96-hour grace balance. While the balance is positive, late responses
earn full credit. Once it reaches zero, a response pushed within one week of
the deadline earns half credit (0.5) and later ones earn 0. Medical
documentation is the only exception. See the syllabus.
